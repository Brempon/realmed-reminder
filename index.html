Questions, answered

Is RealMed Reminder free?

Yes, it is free to download from Google Play.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not at the moment. RealMed Reminder is currently available for Android only.

What happens if I miss a dose?

The app can automatically mark a dose as missed after a time you choose (30 minutes by default), so your adherence stats stay accurate.

Can I back up my medication data?

Yes. Export a backup file to your Downloads folder and restore it later from the same screen.

Can the app replace advice from my pharmacist?

No. Interaction warnings are for information only. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist about your medication.
