What is medication adherence, and how can you improve it?

Adherence simply means taking your medication the way it was prescribed.

What it covers

Taking the right dose, at the right time, for as long as you were told to. Missing doses, taking them late, or stopping early all count as lower adherence.

Why it matters

Many medications work best when taken consistently. If you are struggling with your routine, tell your doctor or pharmacist. They can often adjust the schedule or suggest options that fit your day better.

Common reasons doses get missed

Busy mornings, changes in routine, complicated schedules, running out of pills, and simply forgetting.

How to improve it

Link doses to daily habits, use reminders, keep a refill plan, and review your routine regularly. Tracking helps too, because patterns show up quickly: maybe evening doses are the ones you miss.
