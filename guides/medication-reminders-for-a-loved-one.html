How to help a parent or loved one keep track of their medication

Support works best when the person stays in charge of their own routine.

Start with a conversation

Ask what is hard about the routine and what kind of help they would welcome. People are more likely to stick with a system they helped choose.

Keep one up-to-date list

Write down each medication, dose, and time of day. Bring the list to appointments and update it whenever something changes. A pharmacist can help you check it.

Use reminders that suit them

Some people like a gentle notification, others need a louder alarm. A home screen widget that shows the next dose can help too.

Plan refills early

Set a refill alert so a new supply is ordered before the old one runs out.

Respect privacy

Medication information is personal. Only share it with people they are comfortable with, and keep backup files somewhere safe.
