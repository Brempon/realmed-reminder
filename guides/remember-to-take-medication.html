7 ways to remember to take your medication every day

Missed doses are common, and a few small habits make them much less likely.

1. Tie it to something you already do

Take your pill with brushing your teeth, your morning coffee, or dinner. An existing habit is a reliable cue.

2. Use phone reminders

A dedicated app such as RealMed Reminder can handle several medications, flexible schedules, and refill alerts, which a basic alarm clock handles poorly.

3. Use a weekly pill organizer

Fill it once a week. A glance tells you whether today's dose is already taken.

4. Keep medication where you will see it

Store it somewhere visible and suitable for the medication. Check the label for storage instructions, and keep it away from children.

5. Plan for travel and changes in routine

Pack extra doses and keep reminders on when your schedule or time zone changes.

6. Know what to do after a missed dose

The right answer depends on the medication. Check the leaflet or ask your pharmacist, and do not double up unless a professional tells you to.

7. Ask for help

A family member can check in, and your pharmacist can sometimes simplify your schedule or suggest packaging.
